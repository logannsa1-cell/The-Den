How much should I upload?

Around 10,000 words is a useful default per set; 1,000–10,000 words is a comfortable range.

  • Short revision notes: 100–500 words
  • One lecture: 500–2,000 words
  • Detailed topic: 2,000–5,000 words
  • Full chapter: 3,000–8,000 words
  • Extensive exam notes: 8,000–15,000+ words

These are estimates, not limits. Diagrams, equations, tables and bullets can make a long document relatively short in words. Larger notes may work within the 15 MB per-file and 180,000-character combined limits, subject to processing limits.
